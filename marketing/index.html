One session. More than one view.

Attach without changing another Mac's selected tab. Multiple viewers can watch; Take Control transfers typing and resizing to one person at a time.

↻

Built for the lid-close moment.

Your session stays on its original Mac while your viewing laptop sleeps. Wake your laptop and reconnect over your LAN or VPN to continue.

⌂

At home on your network.

LAN connections stay local and work without internet. Away from home, connect your own VPN. Save an internal address when discovery does not cross the VPN.

The execution Mac must stay powered on, awake, and logged in. Sessions survive app exits and network interruptions, but end on reboot, logout, or session-host failure. Files stay on their workstation.

CLAUDE ACCOUNTS

Your subscriptions.
Ready on your Macs.

One account list for This Mac and the Macs you choose to sync. Select a subscription for the tab you're viewing, whether its shell runs here or on another workstation.

SIGN IN ONCE TO SHARE

Less signing in. More getting on.

Enable Claude sync on both Macs, then Share Sign-In once per subscription. The official Claude Code sign-in creates a reusable token stored in each approved Mac's Keychain.

Add an account while away.

A new reusable sign-in works on that Mac immediately. When LAN or VPN returns, it reaches your approved Macs automatically. Received accounts work even while the originating Mac is offline. Approved Macs can pass shared accounts to their other explicitly approved sync Macs.

Keep the right account in the right tab.

Use the account picker in the status bar. Your choice applies to the next Claude launch in that tab; other tabs and already running Claude processes keep their account.

Ordinary local sign-ins need a one-time Share Sign-In before they can sync. Claude.ai connectors need a normal local sign-in.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Private connections.
Between your Macs.

Your terminals connect directly to paired Macs over your LAN or VPN. You choose which devices connect and which accounts they share.

Read the security model↗
01

Recognize the Mac you trust.

Compare the full fingerprints and approve on both Macs. Connections use TLS 1.3 with mutually verified, pinned device keys.

02

Choose which accounts you share.

Pairing allows workstation access. You approve Claude and AWS sharing separately on both Macs.

03

Stay in control of access.

A paired Mac has powerful access to terminals and tools under your macOS user. Remove a Mac to block it here. Stopping sync blocks additional transfers; revoke credentials at the provider to invalidate existing copies.

Security depends on verified pairing, correct implementation, and uncompromised Macs. Claude requests and software updates have their own network activity; terminal connectivity goes directly to your configured Macs.

A FEW PRACTICAL DETAILS

Clear answers.
Fewer surprises.

Do I need an account to use TerminalDB?

TerminalDB works without an account. Start a local terminal immediately and pair your other Macs when you choose. Claude and AWS features use your existing provider accounts and need internet for provider sign-in and requests.

What happens when I close my laptop?

Its views disconnect. Work on another awake, logged-in Mac continues in that Mac's session host. Wake your laptop and restore LAN or VPN connectivity to reconnect. A sleeping execution Mac cannot keep commands running; reboot, logout, or session-host failure ends its sessions.

Can I use this away from my LAN?

Yes, through your own VPN with a route to the execution Mac's private network. Pair initially on the same LAN. If Bonjour discovery does not cross your VPN, save the paired Mac's internal IP address or internal DNS name in its Connection settings. TerminalDB does not set up your VPN, forward router ports, relay traffic, or synchronize files.

How do accounts sync when I return home?

A reusable Claude sign-in works on the Mac where you add it and syncs to your approved Macs when LAN or VPN connectivity returns. Renewals and removals also sync on reconnect. Ordinary local Claude sign-ins need the one-time Share Sign-In flow before they can sync.

What if two Macs have different AWS profiles, or the same profile name?

TerminalDB detects each Mac's supported profiles. Shared identity includes the provider, account, and role or principal; a matching name alone is not enough. On a receiving Mac, keep a familiar name or choose an alias. Review conflicts before a local profile changes. Unrelated profiles stay untouched.

Can AWS access work while the source Mac is offline?

Yes, until its temporary credentials expire. Renewal needs a reachable Mac with a valid sign-in. Otherwise, use Sign In on This Mac. Receiving tools must support AWS's credential_process interface. A managed same-name profile uses TerminalDB's Sign In button; a separate alias preserves your original AWS login command.

Does unpairing revoke every account credential?

No. It blocks that Mac on this host and stops future transfers. It cannot erase an offline copy or revoke a token already held by another process. Revoke access with Claude or AWS when immediate invalidation is required. Pairing with one Mac does not authorize access to all of its peers.

How does Claude account sharing work?

TerminalDB shares explicitly enabled reusable tokens, stored in each approved Mac's device-only Keychain. Ordinary rotating Claude login and refresh credentials stay local. Claude.ai connectors require a normal local sign-in. Token renewal may require restarting Claude, and provider-side expiry or revocation applies.
